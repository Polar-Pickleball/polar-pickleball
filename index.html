Ottawa has a Pickleball Problem. We're Here to Help.

Pickleball grew by 40% in Canada last year, but the supply of pickleball courts in Ottawa hasn't kept up. Local players are stuck between endless waitlists, shared community gyms, and low quality court alternatives.

We decided to stop waiting and do something about it. We're bringing pickleball court capacity to the City by unlocking innovative partnerships and opportunities in Ottawa. If you'd like to learn more, or want to know about how you can be involved, fill out the survey or send us an email!
