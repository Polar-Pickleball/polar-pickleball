Ottawa has a Pickleball Crisis. The City Cannot Solve It Alone.

The sport is growing at 37% every year, but Ottawa has only ONE new public indoor facility planned for the next decade. For six months of the year, our outdoor courts are unusable, and players are trapped fighting for scraps of time in shared community gyms.

It’s time to stop waiting and start building. Polar Pickleball is forming a coalition of players, property developers, and investors to unlock innovative partnerships and shared commercial spaces. We have the demand. We need the space. Tell us how you fit into the solution.

Collaborative Architecture

Standard municipal funding cannot shoulder this deficit alone. The solution requires strategic alliances and the adaptive reuse of existing commercial real estate. By matching private capital and underutilized commercial footprints with localized player demand, Polar Pickleball is bypassing bureaucratic delays to collaboratively engineer the winter infrastructure Ottawa requires.
